Services

Modular by design: start with a small, low-risk engagement and scale up only if the work earns it.

Report

Development critique

A written deep-dive on your prototype: what the game is trying to do, where it breaks, and prioritized fixes — each costed in complexity and components.

Best before pitching or signing.

Testing

Blind playtest coordination

Structured sessions built around a defined question, observed without steering, and synthesized into evidence — what players did, not just what they said.

Best for pre-production titles.

Editing

Rulebook editing

Terminology discipline, teach-in-play-order structure, edge cases found before your players find them. A rulebook that carries the game on its own.

Best 1–2 months before print.

Production

Insert & organizer design

The inside of the box, designed like the building it is: trays and organizers that fit your components, speed up setup, and survive shelf storage. Prototyped in-house on a 3D printer, so you hold a working sample — not a drawing.

Best during production planning.

Full service

Full development

From signed prototype to shippable game: balancing, cutting, testing cycles, and rules polish — within your budget, your audience, and your deadline.

Scoped per project.

About

I'm Shahdad "Buddieh" Neshin, a game developer and designer based in Ghent, Belgium. By training I'm an architect — which turns out to be excellent preparation for games: both are systems people move through, and both live or die on clear documentation. It's also why I design the inside of the box, not just the game in it. On other people's games I bring that same precision: it's never my game, and my best work is invisible.

I play and analyze 60+ new titles a year across every genre and weight — euros to children's games — and keep a structural log of all of them. That library is what I think with.
